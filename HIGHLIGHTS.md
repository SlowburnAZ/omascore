# OmaScore — highlights

- **15 leagues**: NFL, CFB, NBA, WNBA, NCAAM, NCAAW, MLB, NHL, MLS, EPL, LaLiga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, UCL
- **Live bar widget**: star + score the moment a favorite's game starts, rotates through simultaneous games, full line in the tooltip
- **Works across leagues and monitors**: every league you have a favorite in lights the bar on every screen
- **Full panel scoreboard**: league switcher, 7-day selector, team logos, live scores, kickoffs in your timezone, spread + O/U lines
- **Game detail view**: team-colored head-to-head stats, player tables, play-by-play, injuries, standings, live drive tracker for football
- **Notifications**: score, final, and 10-minute kickoff reminders for favorites — exact-once across multi-monitor setups, panel closed or open
- **In-sync everywhere**: favorites and browsing sync across screens instantly, multi-favorite rotation, adaptive 25s–2min polling
- **Keyboard-driven**: `↑/↓` + `Enter` + `Esc`; settings for notifications, odds, hiding finals
- **Hardened by design**: fixed-argv `curl` only (no shell), capped and sanitized responses, https-only links, ESPN-CDN-only media
- **Zero setup**: ESPN public API, no keys, no accounts, favorites persist in dconf

```
omarchy plugin add https://github.com/SlowburnAZ/omascore.git --enable
```
